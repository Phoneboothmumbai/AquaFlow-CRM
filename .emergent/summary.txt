<analysis>**original_problem_statement:**
The user wants to build a Swimming Pool Service Management Web Portal for a company named Graand Prix. This evolved into a full multi-tenant SaaS platform.

**PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS:**

*   **Core Functionality:** A web-based portal for technicians (engineers), back-office, and management to track Annual Maintenance Contract (AMC) services.
    *   Engineers log in to see their daily schedule.
    *   **Proof of Work:** System must capture start/end time and GPS location automatically when an engineer starts/ends a job.
    *   During a service, engineers fill a checklist, record readings (chlorine, pH), add remarks, and optionally upload photos.
    *   Dashboards for management to see live service status, who is working, where, and any delays.
*   **Portals:**
    *   **Company Portal (Admin):** Manage customers, pools, AMCs, schedules, and engineers.
    *   **Engineer Portal:** View assigned jobs, perform start/end work actions, and fill service details.
    *   **Customer Portal:** View AMC details, service history, and proof of work.
    *   **Super Admin Portal:** (Planned for a later phase) To manage tenant companies and subscription plans.
*   **Sales CRM & Project Execution Module:**
    *   Manage a sales pipeline from lead capture to quotation.
    *   Convert approved quotes into work orders.
    *   Track project execution through stages: BOQ, Procurement, Material Delivery, Installation, Testing, Commencing.
    *   A visual timeline for work orders.
    *   Ability to add comments and assign tasks to employees for each work order.
*   **Technical Decisions:**
    *   **Authentication:** JWT-based custom auth.
    *   **Photo Storage:** Deferred. Initially planned for customer-linked cloud storage (Google Drive/OneDrive), but postponed to focus on core workflow.
    *   **Reports:** PDF format.
    *   **Notifications:** In-app only for now. Email/WhatsApp planned for a later phase.
    *   **Branding:** Subscribers should be able to customize their branding from their panel.

**User's preferred language**: English
